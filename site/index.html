BlackHole installation guide
1

Set up your Mac

You need an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later. For calls, install BlackHole 2ch. To also translate the meeting, install BlackHole 16ch.

2

Add your API keys

Paste your keys in Settings › Accounts. You need an OpenAI key; ElevenLabs and Fish Audio are optional. Each service bills its own API use. ChatGPT and other consumer plans do not include API credits.

Manage OpenAI API keys
3

Choose your route

Translate both sides of a Teams call

Your voice: choose Virtual microphone, your microphone, and BlackHole 2ch as output. In Teams, choose BlackHole 2ch as the microphone.

Meeting audio: in Teams, choose BlackHole 16ch as the speaker. In Babelfin, choose BlackHole 16ch as input and your headphones as output.

Listen to Mac audio or the room

Under Meeting audio, choose your headphones as output. To translate a video or other Mac audio, send it to BlackHole and choose BlackHole as input. To translate a TV or radio in the room, choose your microphone.

Two-way translation needs two separate loopback devices. Babelfin does not change your system audio settings.

4

Start when you are ready

Press Translate in either direction and allow microphone access. Hear chooses the translation, both or the original. Stop translating keeps the original audio; Mute silences the direction.

Your microphone. Your decision.

Babelfin sends audio and text only to the services you choose, under their terms and charges. Translation starts only when you choose it. There is no Babelfin account, no analytics and no recording of live audio. Babelfin contacts GitHub only when you check for updates.

Your keys stay on your Mac. The transcript stays in memory until you save it or quit.
