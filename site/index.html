Speech in. Speech out.

A direct service hears your speech and speaks the translation in one step: OpenAI, Gemini, Palabra or Azure Live Interpreter. Each needs only its own key.

To choose the voice that speaks for you, use ElevenLabs or Fish Audio. ElevenLabs writes down your words, OpenAI translates the text, and your chosen voice speaks it. These routes also let you set your input language and the delay.

Hear it for yourself. Real output, real delay.

Each demo plays generated speech through Babelfin with OpenAI direct translation, so the delay you hear is real. The original audio plays quietly under the translation. For these recordings, each speaker went through a separate session. Choose whose ears you listen with. The people, places and events are fictional.

Use a direct service (OpenAI, Gemini, Palabra or Azure Live Interpreter), or ElevenLabs and OpenAI with an ElevenLabs or Fish Audio voice. Bring your own API keys. One direction costs about $0.50–2.50 per meeting hour in API usage, depending on the service; the README lists estimates for each.

BlackHole installation guide
1

Set up your Mac

You need an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later. For calls, install BlackHole 2ch. To also translate the meeting, install BlackHole 16ch.

2

Add your API keys

Paste your keys in Settings › Accounts. Add a key for each service you use. Each service bills its own API use. ChatGPT and other consumer plans do not include API credits.

Manage OpenAI API keys
3

Choose your route

Translate both sides of a Teams call

Speak: choose Virtual microphone, your microphone, and BlackHole 2ch as output. In Teams, choose BlackHole 2ch as the microphone.

Listen: in Teams, choose BlackHole 16ch as the speaker. In Babelfin, choose BlackHole 16ch as input and your headphones as output.

Listen to Mac audio or the room

Under Listen, choose your headphones as output. To translate a video or other Mac audio, send it to BlackHole and choose BlackHole as input. To translate a TV or radio in the room, choose your microphone.

Two-way translation needs two separate loopback devices. Babelfin does not change your system audio settings.

4

Start when you are ready

Press Translate in either direction and allow microphone access. Hear chooses the translation, both or the original. Stop translating keeps the original audio; Mute silences the direction.

Keep the useful words.

Tools in the sidebar work with recordings and text.

From audio to text

Transcribe a recording or your microphone and translate the text. ElevenLabs can label the speakers. Dub a recording: the translated speech starts at the time of each original passage.

Voice clips and notes

Type a passage or record yourself, then choose the voice, speed and delivery. Compare up to five takes before you save. Give your own recording a new voice, or turn meeting text into short notes.

Your microphone. Your decision.

Babelfin sends audio and text only to the services you choose, under their terms and charges. Translation starts only when you choose it. There is no Babelfin account, no analytics and no recording of live audio. Babelfin contacts GitHub only when you check for updates.

Your keys stay on your Mac. The transcript stays in memory until you save it or quit.
