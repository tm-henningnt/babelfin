Let Babelfin do the translating.

Speak in your language and listen to the meeting in yours. Each direction has its own settings and can run independently.

Before you start

  • In Babelfin → Settings, choose a translation service and save your own keys for the services it uses. Choose local key storage (recommended) or Keychain in Settings → Accounts. Each service bills your account separately; a ChatGPT subscription does not include OpenAI API usage.
  • For virtual microphone or system-audio routes, install BlackHole separately. Follow its installer’s instructions. Babelfin does not install drivers or change system audio defaults.
  • Approve microphone access when asked. If denied, enable Babelfin in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Store your API keys

Keychain encrypts keys and may ask for access after an app update. This Mac (local file) avoids those prompts, but stores keys unencrypted in files restricted to your macOS account. Changing this setting does not move or delete any saved keys. Enter the keys separately in your chosen location.

Use in Teams

  1. Under Speak, choose Virtual microphone, your physical microphone and BlackHole 2ch as output. In Teams → Settings → Devices, choose BlackHole 2ch as the microphone.
  2. To translate the meeting too, install and choose a genuinely separate loopback, such as BlackHole 16ch, as the Teams speaker. Under Listen, select that loopback as input and your headphones as output. BlackHole 16ch uses channels 1 and 2.
  3. Choose the language and service for each direction, then press Translate for either direction. Try a private test call before your first meeting. Teams needs microphone permission too.

The same loopback device or a mirror cannot isolate incoming and outgoing streams. If translating only your voice, keep Teams’ speaker on headphones. Babelfin must keep running for the route to work; closing its window leaves it in the menu bar. Use Off to close a direction, or turn both off from the menu bar.

Hear your own translation

Under Speak, choose My headphones, a microphone and physical headphones. Choose Translate to hear the translation. A physical headset can be both input and output; Bluetooth may change audio quality while its microphone is in use.

Translate a video

  1. Under Listen, choose BlackHole 2ch as source and your headphones as output.
  2. In macOS Sound settings, change the Mac’s output to BlackHole 2ch. Play the video, then choose Translate in Babelfin.
  3. When finished, stop Babelfin and restore your usual macOS output.

This captures all Mac audio sent to BlackHole, including notifications. Babelfin does not isolate a browser tab. The same loopback cannot be both source and output.

Services, languages and voices

OpenAI direct translation receives audio and returns translated speech, with automatic input language detection. The other routes use ElevenLabs to recognize speech, OpenAI to translate the text, and either ElevenLabs or Fish Audio to speak the result. These routes can detect the input language automatically or use your selection. Available target languages depend on the service.

For the ElevenLabs and Fish Audio routes, Delay sets how much speech Babelfin collects before it translates. Fast stays close behind the speaker, More context waits for longer passages, which can translate better, and Balanced is in between. Babelfin adjusts the speed of the translated voice slightly to keep the delay steady.

For ElevenLabs or Fish Audio, refresh and select an account voice in Settings. Choose a physical output to preview it. To create a voice, explicitly choose and upload a sample of a voice you own or have permission to clone. The sample goes to the selected voice provider; audio from Listen is never used as a sample.

Subtitles and text-only translation

Choose Translation › Show Subtitles (Option-Command-U) to show translated speech in a floating window above other apps, also in full-screen meetings. Captions show the latest lines in large text; Transcript shows the running translation. The options button chooses the style, the direction and the text size. Drag the window to move it.

To read a translation instead of hearing it, turn off Speak the translation in a direction’s settings. You then hear the original audio at full volume.

Language setup and your own translation server

Settings › Language setup suggests a service for each direction from your language and the meeting language, and shows which accounts each one needs. In Settings › Accounts › Translation server, an OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama or LM Studio can translate the text for the ElevenLabs and Fish Audio routes.

Hear

Hear chooses what you hear, also while translating: Translation, Both or Original. With Original, translation keeps running and the original plays at full volume. Stop translating returns to the original audio and ends the translation connection. Off stops all audio in the direction.

Timing

Open Timing under a direction’s controls, or choose Show timing in Compact controls. For ElevenLabs and Fish Audio it shows the time for translation, for the voice’s first audio and for waiting while the previous passage plays, and the total from the speaker’s pause to translated speech. OpenAI does all stages in one step, so Babelfin cannot time them separately.

Output volume

Each direction has its own output volume: the slider in the main window and in Compact controls, or Volume in the menu bar menu. It changes only Babelfin’s output for that direction, not the system volume. Use it when macOS output goes to BlackHole and the volume keys no longer control your speakers.

Keep your original voice available

Start original audio in the direction’s options routes the source without translation or a translation-service connection. It needs no API key and is not transcribed. Speech already in the target language may not produce translated audio.

In virtual microphone mode, Ready for calls can watch for another app using BlackHole and pass your original voice through. A microphone preview can count as use; this is not meeting or mute detection. Translation starts only when you choose it. Off or Mute overrides automatic capture.

In Settings, you can mix original audio under translated speech and choose its volume. You can also lower the original while translation plays. Translation arrives later than the source, so the voices are not synchronized.

A smaller control center

Choose Compact controls at the top of the main window. Both directions keep their Translate, Mute and Off controls. Stop translating continues with original audio; Off closes the route. Choose Expand to return to audio setup. Open Meeting transcript to see the text.

Audio tools

Open Audio tools from Babelfin’s menu. For a recording, choose a file up to 25 MB and a transcription service, then press Transcribe to upload it. ElevenLabs can add anonymous speaker labels. Translate the resulting text with OpenAI, then copy or save it.

Meeting notes can use your meeting text or pasted text. Notes are generated only when you ask; review them against the original before relying on decisions or action items. Voice clip turns up to 2,000 characters into a WAV file using an ElevenLabs or Fish Audio voice. Choose Generate, then Play to listen through your selected headphones, Stop to end playback, or save the clip.

Meeting transcript

Open Meeting transcript to see original and translated text from both directions. Copy text with an optional prompt for another chatbot, or save it as plain text or JSON. Babelfin does not send it to a chatbot automatically. The transcript covers periods when translation is active, with gaps while it is off or muted. Text may be incomplete and is not necessarily attributed to individual speakers.

Text stays in memory until you clear it or quit, unless you copy or save it. Babelfin alerts you when the transcript is full; save and clear it to continue collecting text.

If you cannot hear anything

  • Check both directions’ inputs and outputs, then refresh devices. If a selected device disconnects, reconnect it and choose Resume; Babelfin will not choose another device.
  • Watch the input level while speaking. If it stays still, check the microphone and permission.
  • If a translation service fails, original audio continues. Choose Retry when you are ready. Try original audio to check the route. Settings → Troubleshooting can check the OpenAI direct connection using generated silence.
  • Check with a listener or test call that Teams can hear the output.
Privacy: Your selected services process audio or text, and their terms, retention policies and charges apply. Original-only routing uses no translation service; a meeting app can still send your voice to participants. Babelfin does not record your live audio. Clipboard contents and files you choose to save are under your control.
